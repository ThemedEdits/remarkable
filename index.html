FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer Cash on Delivery (COD)?
Yes! We offer Cash on Delivery across all major cities in Pakistan. For Karachi, we also offer same-day delivery. COD is available for orders up to PKR 400,000. For higher-value orders, a 30% advance is required.
Are the laptops covered by warranty?
Absolutely. Every laptop sold at The Remarkable comes with the manufacturer's official warranty. Apple products include 1-year AppleCare. Business laptops from HP, Dell, and Lenovo include their respective official warranties. We also offer extended warranty options at checkout.
What is your return policy?
We offer a 7-day hassle-free return policy. If you're not satisfied with your purchase, contact us within 7 days of delivery. The laptop must be in original condition with all accessories and packaging. Refunds are processed within 3–5 business days.
How long does delivery take?
Karachi: Same-day or next-day delivery. Lahore, Islamabad, Rawalpindi: 1–2 business days. Other major cities: 2–3 business days. Remote areas: 3–5 business days. All orders are fully tracked and insured.
Do you offer installment plans?
Yes! We offer easy installment plans through major Pakistani banks including HBL, MCB, UBL, and more. 0% markup installments are available for up to 12 months on select banks and cards. Contact us via WhatsApp for details.
Can I visit your store in person?
Yes! Our store is located at Shop #14, Tech Bazaar, Saddar, Karachi. We're open Monday through Saturday, 10am to 8pm. You're welcome to come in, try out the laptops, and get personalized advice from our team.
